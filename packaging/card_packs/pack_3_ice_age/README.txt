PACK 3 — ICE AGE

Build locally with: python3 tools/pack_3_ice_age.py
Fetch artwork first with: python3 tools/pack_3_ice_age.py fetch-art
Verify with: python3 tools/pack_3_ice_age.py verify

Copy Pack-3-Ice_Age.zip to the folder opened by Options > Card Packs.
Rescan, then enable Pack 3. In Deck Builder, open Extras and use the Ice Age
On/Off row. The 1997, Pack 1 and Fallen Empires source filters are independent.
Decks remain name-based. Ice Age reprints do not create duplicate identities.

Catalogue: 383 published printings, 373 names, 346 new playable identities and
27 reprints. Both full and cropped artwork are included for the selected
printing of each name; alternate basic-land printings remain in the metadata.

Digital adaptations: Balduvian Shaman changes the named color only on the five
colored Circles of Protection; it cannot rewrite arbitrary enchantment text.
Game of Chaos stops after 30 flips in one resolution. Both adaptations appear
in the digital cards' rules text, Help, and docs/simplified-cards.md.

Only the construction tools and metadata are distributed, never this ZIP or
downloaded card artwork. Card images and characters belong to their owners.
Artwork is fetched from Scryfall for personal local use; respect its policies.
No executable code is accepted from this archive.
