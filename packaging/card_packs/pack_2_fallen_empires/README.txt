PACK 2 — FALLEN EMPIRES

102 unique card names; 187 published printings. Alternate illustrations
share a name-based card identity and the same deck copy limit.

Build locally with tools/pack_2_fallen_empires.py:
  python3 tools/pack_2_fallen_empires.py fetch-art
  python3 tools/pack_2_fallen_empires.py build
  python3 tools/pack_2_fallen_empires.py verify

Place Pack-2-Fallen-Empires.zip in your configured Card Packs folder, then
choose Options > Card Packs > Rescan and enable Pack 2. Pack 1 is optional;
the two packs can be enabled independently.

In Deck Builder, Extras opens the additional-set medallions. Their toggles
filter the card inventory immediately, alongside the original set filters.
Closing Extras keeps the selected filters. Decks remain keyed by card name.

Fallen Empires changes the available deck-building pool. It is not inserted
into original enemy decks automatically. Disable it for the original pool.

Card metadata and art originate from Scryfall. Artwork and this built ZIP
are for local use; releases distribute construction source, not this pack.
The archive contains metadata and images only, never executable code.
